Case-based practice

Case 1 · Photoelectric effect

Light with a frequency above a metal’s threshold frequency shines on its surface. The light intensity is increased while its frequency stays constant.

Part A: Which quantity increases?
Photoelectric current increases because more photons/electrons arrive per second. Maximum kinetic energy depends on frequency, not intensity.
Part B: What if the frequency falls below threshold?
In the ideal photoelectric effect, no electrons are emitted, even if intensity is increased; each photon has less energy than the work function.
Case 2 · Lens in a medium

A convex lens is immersed in a liquid whose refractive index is closer to that of the lens than air is.

What happens to its power and focal length?
The relative refractive-index contrast decreases, so the converging power decreases and the focal length increases. Apply the lens-maker relation using the given indices and sign convention.
